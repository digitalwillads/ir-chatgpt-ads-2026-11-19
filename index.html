ChatGPT ads for ADI, Thomas and Welch

This is the full setup for the ChatGPT test, brand by brand: the account settings, every campaign and ad with its picture, and the conversations each ad shows up in. The ads are written from your approved Google ads and your own pages, and the pictures come from your sites. We build everything paused, so nothing spends until the ads clear OpenAI's review and we turn them on.

Each brand gets two campaigns at $25 a day, the lowest OpenAI allows. ADI and Welch also get product ads for their shops at $25 a day each, once OpenAI switches those on. That's $150 a day to start, plus $50 for the shops, and it comes out of Google campaigns that aren't bringing in leads.

What we need from you

  1. Make the three ChatGPT ads accounts, put the company card on them and invite will@digitalwillads.com. It takes about 15 minutes, and here's how.
  2. Have your web team add our tracking tags in Tag Manager (GTM-5DRHTRFG and GTM-TDXVF8KM). This page has the code and where it goes. We'll send each brand's pixel ID once the accounts exist.
  3. Send an Eloqua ID for each campaign so Skai can count the MQLs. Dmytro will send the campaign names, like he does for Google and Bing.

Tracking

OpenAI's pixel goes on airdimensions.com through Tag Manager (GTM-TDXVF8KM), and it waits for the visitor to accept cookies, same as your Meta pixel. How the test runs

  1. You make the accounts and invite us.
  2. We build everything above in a day or two, paused, and send a test form on each site once your web team has the tags in.
  3. The ads go through OpenAI's review, and we turn them on.
  4. ChatGPT gets its own line in the weekly report, next to Google and Bing.
  5. At 30 days we compare forms, MQLs and cost per form with Google, then keep, change or stop each campaign.

Every campaign starts out bidding for clicks. Once leads build up, we'll add a campaign that bids for leads, since OpenAI doesn't let an existing campaign switch.
