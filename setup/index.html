How to create your ChatGPT ads accounts

About 15 minutes. You make one account for each brand, add the company card and invite Will. We do everything else. See the full ads plan.

1

Sign in at ads.openai.com

Open ads.openai.com and select Sign up (or Log in). Use your irco.com email. If you already use ChatGPT for work, use that same login.

2

Skip the first ad

Ads Manager first asks for a website so it can write an ad for you. Choose the option to skip this step and go to Ads Manager. We build the ads.

3

Tell it about the first brand: ADI

Fill in the business details for ADI. The name you type becomes the account name, the brand name on the ads and the legal name. You can change each one later.

If you see a Tenant menu, choose Ingersoll Rand. If it asks for an administrator's permission, the team that runs ChatGPT at Ingersoll Rand must give you Ads access first.

5

Add the company card

Open Billing and enter the card and the billing details: the business name, the billing address and the email that gets the invoices. OpenAI can put a small temporary hold on the card to check it. No ad runs until a card is on the account, and nothing spends until we turn the campaigns on.

6

Check the names

Open Settings → General. Make the Legal Name your registered company name, the one on the card's billing. Keep the Brand Name as in the table at the top. We upload the logo.

7

Invite Will

Open Settings → Users, select Invite and enter will@digitalwillads.com. Choose the role Admin, so we can build the campaigns and add the tracking.

8

Make the Thomas and Welch accounts

Open the account menu at the top and select Create account. Do steps 3 to 7 again for Thomas, then for Welch, with the names and websites in the table at the top. One login can hold up to 10 ad accounts.

9

Tell us it is done

Reply to Will's email. We see the three accounts as soon as you invite us, and we start the build.
