This takes about 15 minutes. You'll make one account for each brand, put the company card on it and invite Will. We take it from there. The full plan shows everything we'll build.

1. Sign in at ads.openai.com

Go to ads.openai.com and hit Sign up, or Log in if you already use ChatGPT for work. Use your irco.com email.

2. Skip the first ad

It'll ask for a website so it can write you a first ad. Skip that and go straight to Ads Manager. We'll build the ads.

3. Fill in ADI's details

Whatever you put as the advertiser name becomes the account name, the name on the ads and the legal name. You can change each of those later.

If you see a Tenant menu, pick Ingersoll Rand. If it says you need an admin's OK, whoever runs ChatGPT at Ingersoll Rand has to give you Ads access first.

5. Add the card

Open Billing and add the card, the business name and address, and the email that should get the invoices. OpenAI might put a small temporary hold on the card to check it. Nothing runs without a card, and nothing spends until we turn the campaigns on.

6. Check the names

Go to Settings, then General. Put your registered company name in Legal Name, and leave the Brand Name as it is in the table up top. We'll upload the logo.

7. Invite Will

Go to Settings, then Users, hit Invite and add will@digitalwillads.com as an Admin, so we can build the campaigns and set up the tracking.

8. Do the same for Thomas and Welch

Open the account menu at the top and pick Create account. Then do steps 3 to 7 again with the names in the table up top. One login can hold up to 10 accounts.

9. Let Will know

Reply to Will's email once you're done. We'll see the accounts as soon as you invite us, and we'll get started.
